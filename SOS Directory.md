- **CPR:** Call 1990. Push hard and fast in the centre of the chest (100-120 per minute) until help arrives.
- **Bleeding:** Press firmly with a clean cloth, do not remove it, raise the limb if you can.
- **Burns:** Cool with running water for 20 minutes. No ice, no butter.
- **Choking:** 5 firm back blows, then 5 abdominal thrusts. Repeat; call 1990.

This is a quick reminder only, not a replacement for proper first-aid training.
